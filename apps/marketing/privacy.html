Current service disclosure

Privacy notice

Last updated: September 4, 2026

True Canadian Maps is operated by Aello Software. This notice describes the current founding preview. It will be updated when the service, providers or data practices change.

Where the managed service runs

The managed founding preview currently operates on Hostinger infrastructure in Boston, Massachusetts, United States. This includes the application, account and project database, map processing and map delivery used by the managed service. A Canadian managed region is not currently available.

The Linux x64 self-hosted preview runs on infrastructure chosen and managed by its operator. Contact that operator for the privacy practices of their installation.

Information we process

We process information needed to provide, secure and support the service, which may include:

  • account identifiers, authentication and session information;
  • organization, project, style, marker and map content submitted to the service;
  • technical request, security and operational logs; and
  • messages and contact details you send when requesting support or joining the founding program.

Published maps and sessions

Publishing makes map styles, locations and associated assets publicly accessible, including past published releases. Browser-origin restrictions do not make these files confidential. Do not publish sensitive personal information or content you cannot lawfully share. Copies already downloaded by others cannot be recalled.

Studio uses necessary authentication cookies to keep you signed in. Account and unpublished project access requires authentication. Public map requests may generate technical logs on the delivery service.

Marketing website

The public marketing website does not intentionally use third-party analytics or advertising pixels. Normal web-server logs may record technical information such as an IP address, requested URL, browser details and request time for security and operational purposes.

Foreign jurisdiction

Information processed by the managed service in the United States is subject to applicable U.S. law and may be accessible to courts, law-enforcement or national-security authorities through lawful processes in that jurisdiction.

Service providers

Hostinger provides the infrastructure for the current managed preview. Resend delivers account emails, such as password resets, using its U.S. sending region. It processes recipient email addresses, names and message contents, including temporary account links. Email providers also process messages sent to or from our contact addresses. We do not sell personal information or use it for third-party advertising.

Retention and your choices

We retain information only as needed to provide and secure the preview, meet legal obligations and resolve disputes. You may ask to access, correct or delete your information. Some information may need to be retained where required for security, legal or operational reasons.

Contact

Questions or requests about privacy can be sent to privacy@aellosoftware.com.
