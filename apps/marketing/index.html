Founding roadmap · Place-name governance

Every important label should answer: “according to whom?”

The planned governance layer will build policies from official authorities, language preferences and customer-approved exceptions, storing stable source identifiers instead of brittle text replacements.

  • English, French and multilingual name fields
  • Official, alternate, historical and editorial status
  • Human approval for protected names
  • Diff reports before every data release
  • Source and decision history visible to administrators

Questions

What teams usually ask first

Is this a replacement for Google Maps?

It is a replacement for many branded basemap and location-map use cases. The first product does not attempt to replace global turn-by-turn navigation, live traffic or street-level imagery.

Can we keep our existing location cards and directions links?

Yes. The map component can be replaced independently while the surrounding website, branch pages and external directions links remain unchanged.

Where does the managed service run?

The managed founding preview currently runs in Boston, Massachusetts, United States, and data processed there may be subject to U.S. law. A Canadian managed region is not currently available. With the Linux x64 self-hosted preview, you choose and manage your own hosting location.

Are published maps private?

No. Published styles, locations, map assets and previous releases have public URLs. Allowed browser origins are not an access-control boundary for those files. Publish only content you have permission to make public.

Can we export our work?

You can export a published map as style JSON and related assets, including your location data. Keep independent backups of your work during the preview.

Who decides which place name is correct?

The current preview uses the available basemap name fields and language settings. Documented authority profiles, protected-name approvals and provenance workflows are planned, not implemented.
